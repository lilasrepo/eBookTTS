# OpenCC here is a MERGED two-package install; BOTH are load-bearing:

#                                         mis-picks are patched in textconv._POSTFIX_RULES).

#                                         verify's Simplified-marker precision reads; missing it,
#                                         markers inflate and regress FAILs.

# ordered steps in README.md (Install section): reimplemented FIRST, then OpenCC, so opencc/__init__.py stays the
